Our Philosophy

Made the way you'd make it at home.

At Evelyn's Sandwich Factory, we believe the best food doesn't need to be complicated.

We make our soups, sandwiches and salads with fresh ingredients, locally supplied meats and cheeses, vegetables from local grocers and farmers' markets, and bread from right across the street at Spinosa's Bakery.
